By Road

Jamshedpur and Ranchi are connected via the smooth 4-lane NH-33 (~130 km / 2.5–3 hours). Regular cabs and intercity coaches run frequently between Jamshedpur and Ranchi.

By Train

Tatanagar Junction (TATA) is the prime rail hub of Jamshedpur, conveniently located just 15 minutes from Bishtupur and Kadma. It offers direct superfast connections from Kolkata (Howrah), Delhi, Mumbai, Patna, and Ranchi.

Ranchi Railway Station (RNC) / Hatia is directly connected for guests visiting the wedding venue in Ranchi.

By Air

Birsa Munda Airport, Ranchi (IXR) is the nearest commercial airport (~130 km), connected with frequent daily flights to Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad. Kolkata (CCU) is also well connected by train and road.
